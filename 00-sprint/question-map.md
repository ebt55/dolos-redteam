## How to read this table

- **"Known"** is a claim from at least one paper, not a replicated fact. Check the note's verification log before quoting.
- **"Not"** is the gap that makes a weekend project novel. If a row has no gap that excites you, pick another row.
- **"Best project"** is my ranking, not the organisers'. The full scored list is in `../04-ideas/shortlist.md`; 61 alternatives are in `../04-ideas/idea-bank.md`.
- **Budget** is the organisers' estimate for a pilot, not a cap. Add 30–50% for retries and judge calls.

## The cross-track overlaps worth exploiting

- **1.3 ↔ 2.3:** a dependence statistic is also a plus factor; a detector is also an evidence standard.
- **3.1 ↔ 3.4:** the carrier and the monitor are the same design problem — what is observable, and to whom.
- **1.2 ↔ 3.3:** replacement and peer recognition both test whether a coalition is a relationship or a convention.
- **1.4 ↔ 2.2:** an information-theoretic statistic is a hybrid detector that ignores text entirely.
- **2.1 ↔ 3.2:** the tasks that produce collusion are the tasks where a trusted agent can plausibly join.
