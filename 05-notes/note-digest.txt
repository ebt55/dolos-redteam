SETUP: - **Environment / game:** N-player repeated Bertrand, prices discretised to `{0, 1/K, …, 1}`, zero marginal cost, winner-takes-all (lowest price earns that price, ties split), T rounds. Market price = time-average of the realised minimum price, which is also total seller revenue. - **Agents & models:** no models. AI agents are modelled as playing an **ε-approximate Nash equilibrium in strategy space** (ε = Θ(1/T)), i.e. a fixed repeated-game policy; a "human" is modelled as switching to **any r(T)-regret strategy** (`r(T) = o(T)`). - **N agents / rounds / seeds:** arbitrary N ≥ 2, arbitrary horizon T, arbitrary price grid K; no numeric experiments and no seeds — all results are theorems. - *
FINDINGS:
   - **One defector (median-profit):** market price `O((ln N + 1)/N + ((r(T)+1)/T)·ln N + 1/K + 1/T)` (Theorem 3.3). At N = 2 the tight construction gives `(1+ln 2)/2 ≈ 0.85` of the monopoly price, improving on the prior `2/e ≈ 0.74` bound of Arunachaleswaran et al.
   - **Tight construction (Theorem 3.5):** there is an equilibrium with price 1 whose post-defection price is `(ln N + 1)/N − 3ln N/K − O(√(r(T)ln(N+1)/T))`. So the two theorems pin the price at `Θ((1+ln N)/N)`.
   - **Warm-up (Proposition 3.1):** a simpler equilibrium survives *any* no-regret defection with price ≥ `1/N − 2/K − r(T)/T` — i.e. a defection does not necessarily collapse the price to zero.
   - **Why the median-profit restriction is needed (Proposition 3.2):** if the **highest-profit** player defects, a constructed equilibrium keeps the price at ≥ `1 − 1/K` for any N. Counter-intuitively, regulators should audit no-regret compliance in the *lower*-profit players.
   - **M defectors (Theorem 3.6):** price ≤ `M/e^{M−1}·(1+o(1))` with no equilibrium assumption at all, matched by a lower bound `M/e^{M−1} − O(1/K)` (Proposition 3.7). Prices fall exponentially in the number of defectors.
   - **Defection-aware agents (Theorem 4.2):** if the remaining agents re-equilibrate knowing the defector's strategy, price ≥ `1 − 1/K − r(T)/T` regardless of N — collusion is fully restored.
